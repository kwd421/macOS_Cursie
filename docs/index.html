FAQ

Good to know before you start.

Does Cursie need to stay open?

No. You can close it after applying a pack. When your Mac restarts, the last cursor is restored after you sign in.

Can I use Windows .cur and .ani files directly?

Yes. Import the files, review their cursor roles, and apply them to macOS. Animated .ani files can be previewed first.

How do I restore the default macOS pointer?

Open the macOS Pointer settings from Cursie, then click Reset Colors. Cursie disables automatic reapply first; macOS completes the final reset.

Do I need Terminal commands?

No. Importing, previewing, applying, and opening the macOS reset controls are all handled from the app.
