Cursie 1.0.9

  • Adds one-click restoration of the macOS default cursor and removes automatic login reapply at the same time.
  • Improves ANI frame timing and selects the correct image and hotspot from multi-resolution CUR files.
  • Keeps the app responsive while loading cursor packs and rejects excessively large or malformed cursor files.
  • Improves automatic filename matching and makes the login helper resilient when Cursie moves after installation.
